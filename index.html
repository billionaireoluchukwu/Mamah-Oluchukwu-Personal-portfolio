The Challenge

Customer reviews contain valuable business insights, but when scattered across hundreds of comments they become difficult to interpret. The challenge was to organise, analyse and present this data in a way that supports informed business decisions.

The Solution

I cleaned and analysed the customer review dataset before designing an interactive dashboard that highlights customer sentiment, recurring themes and operational trends. The final solution transforms raw feedback into clear, actionable insights for decision-makers.

My Role

I managed the complete analytics workflow, including data preparation, exploratory analysis, dashboard design, insight generation and the communication of findings through clear visualisations and recommendations.

Outcome

This project strengthened my analytical thinking and demonstrated my ability to turn raw customer data into meaningful business intelligence. It showcases my skills in data analysis, dashboard design, visual communication and translating insights into practical recommendations.

Key Insights

The analysis revealed that food quality and taste consistently drove positive customer experiences, while delays in service and occasional order inaccuracies were the primary sources of dissatisfaction. These findings informed practical recommendations to improve customer satisfaction, streamline operations and strengthen overall service delivery.
