Kitchen ideas

Simple meals from good ingredients.

Recipe ideas help customers imagine what they can cook with Yagazie products.

Quick Egusi Soup Pack

Use ground egusi, dry fish, crayfish, pepper and vegetables. Best served with garri or pounded yam.

Beans and Plantain Bowl

Cook beans with onion, pepper and palm oil, then serve with ripe plantain for a filling lunch.

Tomato Stew Base

Blend fresh tomatoes, peppers and onion. Fry slowly, season well and portion for the week.
